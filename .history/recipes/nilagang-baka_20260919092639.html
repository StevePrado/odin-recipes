Nilagang Baka Recipe

Nilagang Baka is a classic, clear-broth Filipino beef soup made by slow-boiling tough cuts of beef with leafy vegetables, potatoes, and corn until melt-in-your-mouth tender.

What is Nilagang Baka

Core Flavors: Clean, naturally sweet, and savory, derived entirely from the rendered beef fat, bones, whole peppercorns, and onions.

Main Ingredients: Beef shank (bulalo), brisket, or ribs, combined with cabbage, bok choy, potatoes, and sweet corn on the cob.

Serving: Served hot with white rice and a small side saucer of fish sauce (patis) and squeezed calamansi for dipping.

Ingredients

  • Beef Brisket or Shanks: 1kg, cut into large chunks.
  • Onion: 2 medium pieces, halved or coarsely chopped.
  • Whole Black Peppercorns: 1 tablespoon.
  • Potatoes: 2 medium, peeled and quartered.
  • Sweet Corn: 2 ears, cut into 3 pieces each.
  • Bok Choy (Pechay): 1 bunch, base trimmed and leaves separated.
  • Cabbage: 1 small head, cut into wedges.
  • Fish Sauce (Patis): 2 tablespoons, to season.
  • Water: 6 to 8 cups, enough to fully submerge the beef.

Steps

  1. Boil and Clean the Beef (15 mins)
    • Initial boil: Place beef in a deep pot, cover with water, and bring to a boil for 5 minutes.
    • Clean broth: Skim off all the brown scum and foam that rises to the top to keep the broth crystal clear.
  2. Slow Simmer (1 to 2 hours)
    • Add aromatics: Drop in the onions, whole peppercorns, and fish sauce into the pot.
    • Tenderize: Lower the heat, cover, and let it simmer for 1.5 to 2 hours (or 30 minutes in a pressure cooker) until the meat is completely tender.
  3. Cook Root Crops and Corn (15 mins)
    • Add corn and potatoes: Toss in the sweet corn and potatoes into the simmering beef broth.
    • Boil: Cook uncovered for about 10 to 12 minutes until the potatoes are cooked through.
  4. Add Green Veggies and Finish (3 mins)
    • Add leafy greens: Stir in the cabbage wedges and bok choy leaves into the soup.
    • Final cook: Let it simmer for another 2 to 3 minutes until the vegetables are vibrant green and slightly crisp, then remove from heat.
