Tinola Recipe

Tinola is a comforting, ginger-infused Filipino soup traditionally cooked with chicken, wedges of green papaya, and chili pepper leaves in a flavorful broth.

What is Tinola

Core Flavors: Warm, soothing, and deeply aromatic, dominated by fresh ginger, garlic, and onions simmered with fish sauce.

Main Ingredients: Usually features bone-in chicken pieces, native green papaya or chayote (sayote), and fresh leafy greens.

Serving: Best served piping hot in a bowl alongside white rice, especially during rainy days or cold weather.

Ingredients

  • Chicken Pieces: 1kg, bone-in thighs or drumsticks for a richer broth.
  • Ginger: 2-inch piece, sliced into thin strips (julienned).
  • Garlic: 4 cloves, crushed and minced.
  • Onion: 1 medium, chopped.
  • Green Papaya or Chayote: 1 medium, cut into wedge pieces.
  • Chili Pepper Leaves or Malunggay Leaves: 1 cup, tightly packed.
  • Fish Sauce (Patis): 2 tablespoons, plus more to taste.
  • Water or Rice Wash (Hugas-Bigas): 5 to 6 cups.
  • Cooking Oil: 2 tablespoons, for sautéing.

Steps

  1. Sauté the Aromatics (5 mins)
    • Heat oil: In a large pot, heat cooking oil over medium heat.
    • Sauté: Stir-fry the ginger, garlic, and onions until aromatically fragrant and slightly softened.
  2. Sear the Chicken (5 mins)
    • Add chicken: Put the chicken pieces into the pot and cook until the outer layer turns light brown.
    • Season: Pour in the fish sauce and stir well, letting the chicken absorb the flavor for 1 to 2 minutes.
  3. Simmer the Broth (20 mins)
    • Pour liquids: Add the water or rice wash into the pot. Bring the mixture to a rolling boil.
    • Simmer: Skim off any floating scum, cover the pot, lower the heat, and simmer for 20 minutes until the chicken is tender.
  4. Add Vegetables and Finish (10 mins)
    • Cook papaya: Add the green papaya or chayote wedges into the broth. Simmer for 5 to 7 minutes until fork-tender.
    • Add greens: Turn off the heat, add the chili pepper or malunggay leaves, and cover the pot to let the residual heat cook the greens.
